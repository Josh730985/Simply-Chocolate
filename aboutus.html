Simply Chocolate

Simply Chocolate brings chocolate back to what it should be. Crafted with carefully selected organic cocoa, our chocolate is made to celebrate rich flavour, smooth texture, and simple ingredients. Every bar is created with a passion for quality and a love for authentic chocolate, it delivers a delicious experience in every bite you take At Simply Chocolate, we believe that great chocolate doesn't need to be complicated, just simple, satisfying, tasty, and truly enjoyable.

About us and our team

Connor Heyes Founder & Creative Lead "Passionate about creating great chocolate and building a brighter future." Leon James Co-Founder & Development Lead "Turning ideas into something truly special through teamwork and innovation." Josh-Li Atkinson Marketing & Brand Development "Helping Simply Chocolate grow by sharing our story and connecting with customers."

At Simply Chocolate, we believe that great chocolate should be simple, delicious, and made with purpose. Founded by friends Joshua-Li Atkinson, Conner Heyes and Leon James, our business began with a shared passion for chocolate and a vision to create treats that bring people together. What started as a simple idea soon grew into a commitment to using quality ingredients, creating memorable flavours, and promoting a brighter future through responsible choices. Every product we make reflects our values of authenticity, creativity, and sustainability.

The team behind Simply Chocolate is built on creativity, collaboration, and a shared passion for great chocolate. Joshua-Li Atkinson, Leon James, and Connor Heyes work together to bring fresh ideas, quality ingredients, and innovative thinking to every part of the business. From developing products and building the brand to connecting with customers, each team member plays an important role in helping Simply Chocolate achieve its mission of delivering exceptional chocolate while promoting better ingredients, happier people, and a brighter tomorrow. Together, we are committed to creating a brand that combines great taste with positive impact.
